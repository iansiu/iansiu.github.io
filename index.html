Lucky4D Results

Singapore 4D, TOTO and Sweep results in one place.

Lucky4D Results is an independent results and reference app for Singapore 4D, TOTO and Sweep. Follow the latest published results, search draw history, save favourite numbers and receive alerts when results are ready.

Features

  • View the latest published results in one place.
  • Browse and search draw history by draw number, date, number and prize scope.
  • Save favourite numbers and get push alerts when results are available.
  • Listen to live Singapore 4D result broadcasts in the app.
